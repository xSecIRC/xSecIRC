## Our Foundation and Mission

The field of cybersecurity is plagued by entities making education a business. These various entities take advantage of the demand, making tons of money and leaving this field inaccessible due to a lack of free education.

**We are xSecIRC**, a secular international, and gender-neutral community of cybersecurity enthusiasts. Our motives are clear: we are here to provide free education to people interested in information technology, cybersecurity, and programming, and to obsolete the entities making education a business.

### **A Fundamental Human Right**

We firmly believe that education is a fundamental human right for everyone. We are here to ensure that no one has been denied the right to obtain education, no matter what ethnicity, group, caste, creed, religion, and gender the person belongs to; human rights violations shouldn't be tolerated.

We want to revolutionize the world of cybersecurity and free education and bring change in society.

### **Our Focus**

This GitHub organization serves as a central hub for sharing the foundational knowledge necessary for the cybersecurity field. We provide resources focused on three core pillars:
* **Information Technology (IT):** Building a solid base for understanding technology.
* **Cybersecurity:** Providing courses and resources in Cybersecurity.
* **Programming:** Focusing on essential languages like Python and Bash scripting.

Our repositories contain curated links and open-source projects designed to help you get started in IT if you are new to the field.

### How To Join the Community

If you want to learn more about information technology, cybersecurity, and programming, or if you want to join a community of like-minded individuals who are committed to making education accessible to all, then come join us!
